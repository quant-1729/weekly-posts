# Stage 1: User stories (global version)

Media, Content & Digital Experiences track. Brands & marketing personas: SMB owner, in-house marketer, agency/freelancer, D2C/e-commerce brand team. Every row traces to the pain-point tables in chat; nothing invented.

This is a separate version from PostMitra (`Projects/Post`). Changes from that version: not India-focused, and all four personas are in scope (D2C means online-first brands, not offline sellers).

| ID | As a (user) | I want | So that | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US-1 | SMB owner / founder | to know what to post each day | I stop guessing and copying competitors | #9 Plan: doesn't know what to post |
| US-2 | SMB owner / founder | content that is tied to a goal like leads or footfall | posting is not just something everyone says I should do | #9 Plan: no strategy or goal |
| US-3 | SMB owner / founder | to create posts without design or editing skills or extra time | content doesn't become a second job | #9 Create: no skill or time |
| US-4 | SMB owner / founder | my posts to look consistent | my business looks credible, not amateur | #9 Create: quality is inconsistent |
| US-5 | SMB owner / founder | content in the language and tone my customers use | my customers actually respond to it | #9 Create: language and tone mismatch |
| US-6 | SMB owner / founder | to post regularly across my social channels, messaging status and Google Business Profile | I don't have gaps of days or weeks | #9 Publish: inconsistent posting |
| US-7 | SMB owner / founder | my Google Business Profile and reviews kept up to date and answered | I show up well in local search | #9 Publish: GBP and reviews neglected |
| US-8 | SMB owner / founder | to see whether my posts bring enquiries or sales | I know if it is working beyond likes | #9 Measure: can't tell if it's working |
| US-9 | SMB owner / founder | clear, visible results for what I spend | I don't waste money like with past agencies | #9 Money: fear of wasted spend |
| US-10 | In-house marketer | to produce enough content for every channel with a small team | I am not stuck prioritising the loudest stakeholder | #10 Plan: volume vs. team size |
| US-11 | In-house marketer | planning and approvals in one place | late feedback doesn't blow up timelines | #10 Plan: calendar and approvals chaos |
| US-12 | In-house marketer | all content to follow one brand voice and look | freelancers and agencies stay on-brand | #10 Create: brand consistency |
| US-13 | In-house marketer | to turn one asset (webinar, blog) into many | content isn't under-used | #10 Create: repurposing is manual |
| US-14 | In-house marketer | AI output that is on-brand and accurate enough to publish | I don't rewrite every AI draft | #10 Create: AI output needs heavy editing |
| US-15 | In-house marketer | one message adapted to each channel's format | I stop copy-pasting and resizing by hand | #10 Publish: channel-wise adaptation |
| US-16 | In-house marketer | fast, good-quality localised versions of campaigns for other markets and languages | localisation isn't slow, costly or uneven | #10 Publish: localisation |
| US-17 | In-house marketer | reporting pulled together from all my channels | monthly reports don't take days | #10 Measure: fragmented reporting |
| US-18 | In-house marketer | to show what content brought in | I can prove ROI to leadership | #10 Measure: proving content ROI |
| US-19 | In-house marketer | fewer overlapping tools that the team actually uses | we stop paying for and losing workflows | #10 Org: tool sprawl and cost |
| US-20 | Agency / freelancer | clear client briefs with goals and assets on time | I avoid long threads and repeat calls | #11 Intake: poor client briefs |
| US-21 | Agency / freelancer | each client's voice, audience and calendar kept separate and handy | I don't mix up or forget client details | #11 Plan: per-client context switching |
| US-22 | Agency / freelancer | fewer, clearer revision rounds in one place | I don't burn out on scattered subjective feedback | #11 Create: revision loops |
| US-23 | Agency / freelancer | to take on more output without more headcount | quality and margin don't drop | #11 Create: scaling output |
| US-24 | Agency / freelancer | resizing, captioning, subtitling and hashtags done per platform per client | I skip repetitive formatting work | #11 Create: repetitive formatting |
| US-25 | Agency / freelancer | client sign-off before posting without chasing | schedules don't break | #11 Publish: multi-client approvals |
| US-26 | Agency / freelancer | monthly client reports without manual copying | I don't lose hours of unpaid work | #11 Measure: reporting time sink |
| US-27 | Agency / freelancer | to show clients the value of my work | they don't churn when results are slow | #11 Business: proving value |
| US-28 | Agency / freelancer | a way to control and bill extra changes | scope creep doesn't eat my margin | #11 Business: pricing and scope creep |
| US-29 | Agency / freelancer | to position my work against "AI can do this" | my rates aren't pushed down | #11 Business: tool and AI pressure |
| US-30 | D2C / e-commerce brand team | copy, images and variations for a large catalogue | every SKU is covered without bulk freelancers | #12 Plan: catalogue scale |
| US-31 | D2C / e-commerce brand team | accurate, unique product descriptions | SEO and conversion improve | #12 Create: PDP quality |
| US-32 | D2C / e-commerce brand team | a steady flow of fresh ad creative variations | performance doesn't drop from creative fatigue | #12 Create: ad creative fatigue |
| US-33 | D2C / e-commerce brand team | UGC and influencer sourcing, briefs, approvals and rights in order | it isn't chaotic | #12 Create: UGC and influencer dependence |
| US-34 | D2C / e-commerce brand team | product visuals without costly, slow shoots | assets don't go stale | #12 Create: shoot cost |
| US-35 | D2C / e-commerce brand team | content formatted for each marketplace and channel | I stop reformatting by hand | #12 Publish: multi-channel formats |
| US-36 | D2C / e-commerce brand team | affordable localised listings and ads | we can reach new markets and languages | #12 Publish: localisation |
| US-37 | D2C / e-commerce brand team | to tie creatives and content to ROAS, CAC and repeat purchase | I know what drives revenue | #12 Measure: attribution gaps |
| US-38 | D2C / e-commerce brand team | creative insights fast enough to act on | learning loops aren't too slow | #12 Measure: slow learning loops |
| US-39 | D2C / e-commerce brand team | volume without losing brand consistency | performance and brand teams stop clashing | #12 Org: speed vs. brand |
