# Stage 3: Solutions catalogue (global version)

What people use today to cope with the Stage 1 pains, across all four personas, globally. Catalogue only; no scoring yet.

**Research caveats**
- Reddit could not be fetched, and search engines returned no Reddit threads. The most first-hand evidence here comes from G2, Capterra and Trustpilot user reviews, which are self-selected and sometimes incentivised.
- Stats from vendor blogs are marked "vendor claim". Rows marked "known tool, not researched this run" come from general knowledge and were not checked.
- Real user interviews are still needed before Stage 5.

## Patterns across the catalogue

1. **Brand voice is the most repeated failure.** Jasper, Predis and Ocoya users and the AI-content guides all report generic output and heavy editing. Tools that solve it (Typeface, Writer) are enterprise-only. SMBs, small agencies and lean teams have a gap.
2. **Approval tools exist, but clients don't adopt them.** The pain is client-side adoption and per-seat pricing, not missing features. This supports the no-login, one-link idea from Stage 2.
3. **Repurposing is fragmented by format.** Video clippers, carousel makers, catalogue writers and dubbing tools are separate products, each with its own brand kit.
4. **Non-software coping is common.** Batching, voice docs, AI-then-rewrite and done-for-you subscriptions are what many people do today.
5. **Crowded at the ends, thinner in the middle.** Cheap generators serve SMBs and governed platforms serve enterprises. Lean marketing teams, small agencies and D2C brands have little that is both on-brand and affordable.
