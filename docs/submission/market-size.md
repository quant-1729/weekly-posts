## TAM / SAM / SOM for India (our estimate)

The price is not decided. This uses an **assumed price of USD 10 a month (USD 120 a year)**, the low end of the internal USD 10-30 a month target (`docs/v2-pipeline/5-challenge.md`). It mixes two different bases (Udyam registrations and a SIDBI survey share), so treat it as a rough order of magnitude.

- **TAM (our estimate)** = Udyam-registered enterprises x annual price = 79 million x USD 120 = **about USD 9.5 billion a year**
- **SAM (our estimate)** = TAM base x share already doing social media marketing = 79 million x 13% = 10.3 million businesses x USD 120 = **about USD 1.2 billion a year**
- **SOM (our estimate, 3 years)** = 0.1% of SAM businesses = about 10,300 paying businesses x USD 120 = **about USD 1.2 million a year**

The 0.1% capture rate is our assumption, not sourced. The global category (#10) is USD 29.9 billion today.

## Slide copy

**Problem size (35 words):**

- 7.9 crore Indian enterprises registered; MSMEs are 99.8% of firms in developing Asia.
- Only 13% of Indian MSMEs use social media marketing.
- 56% of small businesses get an hour or less a day for marketing.

Footnote: MSME Ministry via IANS, 2026; ADB Asia SME Monitor 2025; SIDBI via Entrepreneur India, 2025; Constant Contact, 2024 (US/UK/AU/CA).

**Comparison sentence:**

A freelancer to run a shop's social media in India costs ₹15,000-50,000 a month (upGrowth, 2026), close to or above the ₹17,821 average monthly salary of a social media specialist (Indeed India, 2026).
