Yoga that feels like a deep breath

Welcome to Blue Wave Yoga, a small studio in Surry Hills for people who want to move, stretch and switch off. Whether you have never touched a mat or you have been practising for years, there is a class with your name on it.

We keep classes small, with no more than 12 people, so every teacher can see what you are doing and help you find your edge safely.

Find your flow

  • Lunchtime basics for desk-bound shoulders
