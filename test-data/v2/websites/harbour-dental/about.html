About our dentists

Harbour Dental was founded by local dentists who wanted a practice where families could be seen on time and treated with respect. We are licensed by the Oregon Board of Dentistry and follow all state infection-control rules.

Dr. Miriam Okafor, DDS

Lead dentist. Miriam has been caring for patients for 14 years in practice, with a focus on preventive care and cosmetic work for adults.

Dr. Tomas Reyes, DMD

Associate dentist. Tomas has 9 years in practice and leads our crowns, bridges and implant planning.

Both dentists are available for a consultation before any treatment. Ask us about our approach when you book your visit.
