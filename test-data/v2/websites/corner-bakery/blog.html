From the bakehouse

How to use up stale sourdough

Day-old sourdough makes the best croutons, a silky panzanella and a bread-and-butter pudding that will make your Sunday. Tear it, toss it in olive oil and bake until golden.

Keeping a starter alive over the holidays

Our starter has survived more than one bank holiday. The trick is a weekly feed, a cool cupboard and a friend who is happy to take it home for a fortnight.

Why we bake the cinnamon buns by hand

Machines are fast, but a hand-rolled bun has a softer swirl and a better crumb. It is slower work, and we think it shows.
