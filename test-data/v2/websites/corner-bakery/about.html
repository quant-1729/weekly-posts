About Corner Bakery

We opened our doors in 2009 with one oven, a secondhand mixer and a stubborn belief that a neighbourhood deserves a proper bakery. Seventeen years on, we still bake everything on site.

Our head baker, Ruth Adeyemi, looks after the sourdough starter that has been running since our first week. The bakers start before 4am so the shelves are full when you walk in.

We are a small team on Lansdowne Parade, and most of our regulars know us by name. We would love to know yours.

What we believe

  • Slow fermentation tastes better, even when it takes longer.
  • Good flour and honest recipes come first.
  • A bakery should be a place where you can stop for a minute.

Want to see the bakehouse for yourself? Pop in during opening hours and ask for Ruth. She is usually happy to show you the proving room, as long as the croissants are not waiting.
