Fresh bread, every morning on Lansdowne Parade

Welcome to Corner Bakery, your neighbourhood bakery in Camberwell. We bake our sourdough slowly, shape it by hand and fill the shelves before the school run starts.

Baked on site every morning since 2009. That's our promise to the people who have been queuing at our door for years.

10% off for orders over £30 on Fridays. Ordering for the office, a birthday or a weekend table? Pop in or give us a call and we'll sort it out.

Why people come back

  • Baked on site every morning since 2009. Our ovens are fired up before most of the street is awake.
  • Every sourdough is proved for 24 hours. That slow rise gives a crisp crust and an open, chewy crumb.

Come and see us

Find us at 27 Lansdowne Parade, London SE5 8QJ. Our opening hours are on the contact page, and you can see what we're baking on the menu.

Some of our favourites: the sourdough, butter croissants and the cinnamon buns, which are a Saturday tradition for half of Camberwell.
